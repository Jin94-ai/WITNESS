## The headline finding

> **Cast augmentation alone (1 outsider → 3 outsiders) flips 2-acc-diff-roles
> recovery rate from 0% to 93%.**

Source: [ITER_118_CAST_RESCUE_CONFIRMED.md](../../archive/b_direction_legacy/iter_91_to_119/ITER_118_CAST_RESCUE_CONFIRMED.md) (archived 2026-04-27)
verified at N=15 with proper PYHASH (Iter 105 fix).

This is the strongest causal intervention finding from the
26-iteration empirical arc (Iter 105-130). It validates the
predictive model:

> recovery_rate ≈ baseline × ∏_{r ∈ accused_roles} P(role r forgiven)
>                × (1 - ambient_accusation_load)

Where P(role r forgiven) ≈ 1 if cast has ≥2 role-r agents, ≈ 0 if
cast has 1 role-r agent.
